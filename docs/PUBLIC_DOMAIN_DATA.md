# Public Domain Legal Data & Open Legal Information Framework

This document sets forth the legal, constitutional, and copyright foundations of the legal information, statutory texts, court rules, and judicial opinions utilized and distributed by Legal-GPT.

---

## 1. Federal Law is in the Public Domain

Under United States copyright law, all works created by the United States Government—including federal statutes (United States Code), federal court rules, and federal judicial opinions—are in the public domain and cannot be copyrighted:

- **17 U.S.C. § 105**: "Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government..."
- **17 U.S.C. § 101**: Defines a "work of the United States Government" as work prepared by an officer or employee of the United States Government as part of that person's official duties.
- **U.S. Copyright Office Compendium (Third Edition) § 313.6(C)(2)**: Reaffirms that edicts of government, such as judicial opinions, administrative rulings, legislative enactments, and public ordinances, are not copyrightable for reasons of public policy.
- **Banks v. Manchester, 128 U.S. 244 (1888)**: The Supreme Court established that the judicial decisions of courts and the text of laws are free for all to copy and distribute.

## 2. State Statutes, Case Law, and Edicts of Government

Under the landmark United States Supreme Court decision in **Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc., 140 S. Ct. 1498 (2020)**, the "government edicts doctrine" applies equally to the states: no state or municipal government can assert copyright in the law itself, including statutory codes created by legislators or judges acting within their official capacities.

Below is the public domain and official terms-of-use documentation for the core verified jurisdictions integrated into Legal-GPT:

### Washington (WA)
- **Official Source**: Washington State Legislature (`leg.wa.gov` / `app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. The Office of the Code Reviser and the Washington State Legislature make Revised Code of Washington (RCW) and Washington Administrative Code (WAC) texts freely accessible to the public without restrictive licensing.

### California (CA)
- **Official Source**: California Office of Legislative Counsel (`leginfo.legislature.ca.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain under Cal. Gov. Code § 10248, requiring legislative information to be published on the Internet for uninhibited public use.

### Texas (TX)
- **Official Source**: Texas Legislature Online (`statutes.capitol.texas.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Texas statutory codes and constitutional texts are public government records accessible without restriction.

### New York (NY)
- **Official Source**: New York State Senate & Consolidated Laws (`nysenate.gov/legislation`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. New York Consolidated Laws are official public legislative acts.

### Illinois (IL)
- **Official Source**: Illinois General Assembly (`ilga.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Illinois Compiled Statutes (ILCS) are public government edicts.

### Ohio (OH)
- **Official Source**: Ohio Legislative Service Commission (`codes.ohio.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Ohio Revised Code (ORC) is published for free public access.

### Florida (FL)
- **Official Source**: Florida Legislature Online Sunshine (`leg.state.fl.us`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain under Florida's broad Government in the Sunshine laws and Article I, Section 24 of the Florida Constitution.

### Pennsylvania (PA)
- **Official Source**: Pennsylvania General Assembly (`palegis.us`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes (Pa.C.S.) are official public records.

### Georgia (GA)
- **Official Source**: Georgia General Assembly (`legis.ga.gov` & LexisNexis free portal)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain following *Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc.*, 140 S. Ct. 1498 (2020).

### North Carolina (NC)
- **Official Source**: North Carolina General Assembly (`ncleg.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. North Carolina General Statutes (NCGS) are public legislative documents.

### Michigan (MI)
- **Official Source**: Michigan Legislature (`legislature.mi.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain under the Michigan Freedom of Information Act and state legislative policy.

### New Jersey (NJ)
- **Official Source**: New Jersey State Legislature (`njleg.state.nj.us`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. New Jersey Statutes Annotated (NJSA) legislative text is public.

### Virginia (VA)
- **Official Source**: Virginia's Legislative Information System (`law.lis.virginia.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Code of Virginia is published as an official government edict.

### Arizona (AZ)
- **Official Source**: Arizona State Legislature (`azleg.gov`)
- **Legal Status**: Public domain. Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) are public legislative enactments.

## 5. What Offline Packages Do NOT Contain

Legal-GPT maintains an uncompromising privacy and security perimeter:
- **No Private Case Files**: Packages contain only public laws, court rules, and general literacy concepts.
- **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**: No litigant names, case numbers, addresses, or phone numbers.
- **No Proprietary Databases**: No scraped commercial legal databases or copyrighted annotations.
- **No Local Drive Data**: Never bundles `.env`, local directories, or Evidence Manager vault items.
